# Project Specification: 2D Arcade Career Assessment Game

## Context & Objective
You are an expert game developer and frontend engineer. Your task is to build a 2D HTML5 Canvas web application that serves as a gamified career assessment. The target audience is 15-20 year olds. 

Instead of a traditional quiz, the assessment is disguised as a top-down 2D arcade game. The player controls an avatar in a room. A scenario is presented, and the player must physically move their avatar into one of four colored zones to make their choice.

## Technical Constraints
*   **Tech Stack:** Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript (Canvas API). No external game engines (like Phaser) to keep the bundle lightweight and easily embeddable.
*   **Controls:** WASD or Arrow Keys for desktop movement. (Optional: Add a virtual on-screen joystick for mobile compatibility).
*   **State Management:** The game must manage three states: `START_SCREEN`, `PLAYING_SCENARIO`, and `END_SCREEN`.
*   **Scoring:** The game must track the number of times the player chooses each of the four traits: `Maker`, `Architect`, `Catalyst`, and `Visionary`.

## Game Mechanics & Flow
1.  **Start Screen:** 
    *   Displays the game title: "Crisis Simulator".
    *   Shows a brief instruction: "Use WASD/Arrow keys to move. Run to the zone that matches your instinct."
    *   A "Start Game" button to initiate the loop.
2.  **The Core Loop (Playing):**
    *   The player avatar starts in the center of the canvas.
    *   The current `scenario text` is displayed prominently at the top of the screen.
    *   There are four distinct collision zones (e.g., colored squares or doors) at the Top, Bottom, Left, and Right of the canvas.
    *   Each zone displays short `action text` corresponding to a choice.
    *   When the player's avatar collides with a zone, a visual flash occurs, the choice's associated trait is tallied, and the next scenario loads immediately, resetting the player to the center.
3.  **The End Screen (Loot Drop Reveal):**
    *   After 10 scenarios, the canvas transitions to the results screen.
    *   The system calculates the highest scored trait (or combination of top two).
    *   Displays a "Character Unlock" style result (e.g., "You are: The Systems Master") with a short description of their dominant archetype.

Results Evaluation Logic
At the end of the 10 scenarios, determine the trait with the highest count. Display the corresponding archetype on the End Screen:

Highest Maker: "The Tactical Builder" - You thrive on physical execution, prototyping, and getting your hands dirty to solve immediate problems. (Recommended fields: Engineering, Culinary, Hardware, Trades).

Highest Architect: "The Systems Master" - You excel at structuring chaos, analyzing data, and building logical frameworks. (Recommended fields: Software Dev, Logistics, Data Analysis, Finance).

Highest Catalyst: "The Frontline Leader" - You are a natural at human interaction, empathy, and managing team dynamics under pressure. (Recommended fields: Hospitality, Healthcare, Counseling, Sales).

Highest Visionary: "The Creative Director" - You focus on aesthetics, storytelling, and designing the bigger picture. (Recommended fields: Digital Design, Marketing, Media, Arts).(If there is a tie, concatenate the two archetypes, e.g., "The Tactical Builder / Systems Master").
